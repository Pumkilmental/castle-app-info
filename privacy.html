Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Purpose

Castle is a personal-use utility for retrieving copies of files from an authorized Google Drive account for personal storage and recovery.

Google Drive Access

The application requests read-only Google Drive access to retrieve files, folders, and metadata. It does not modify or delete source files.

Data Storage and Use

Retrieved files are stored on devices controlled by the account holder. Local copies may also be protected by a separately configured third-party backup service, subject to that service's privacy and retention policies.

Authentication

Google authorization credentials are maintained in the account holder's local application configuration. This website does not receive or store Google authorization tokens.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising. The utility does not operate a public server that collects file contents.

Retention and Control

The account holder controls local copies and can delete them. Separately configured backup services may retain historical copies according to their own settings and policies.

Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.

Website

This informational website does not use application-provided analytics, advertising trackers, or data collection forms. The hosting platform may process ordinary website requests.
